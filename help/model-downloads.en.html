Download workflow models

This guide lists the files required by the seven bundled workflows, checked on 26 October 2026. Choose only the workflows you need. LLM/VLM models used to write prompts are separate.

Open “Source and license”, review the terms and sign in if required. Then use “Download file”. Your browser saves the file to its download folder: this guide does not automatically install models. Move it to the listed path relative to ComfyUI/models/, creating subfolders as needed. Keep the filename expected by the workflow. Restart ComfyUI after copying; for customized workflows, use the filenames in their loader nodes.

Licenses, access and attribution

Files remain hosted by their distributors: zPrompt Studio provides links, does not redistribute weights and does not grant model licenses. Ownership and terms remain with each source; public availability does not imply unrestricted commercial use. For gated models each user must obtain access and accept the terms on the source website. Do not put tokens in this guide. Before bundling weights or hosting a copy, check redistribution rights; if they are not granted, permission from the rights holder is required.
