A young elf thief sprints through a dark, mossy mystical forest, clutching a pulsing, glowing emerald gem. Behind her, giant shadow-wolves with bioluminescent red eyes leap between the roots of ancient, twisted trees. She slides under a massive fallen log, grabs a thick vine, and swings across a deep, foggy chasm. The wolves halt at the edge, howling in frustration, as she disappears into the thick white mist.
