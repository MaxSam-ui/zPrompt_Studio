Inside a creaking, dark Victorian house, a woman alone hears a faint scratching sound inside the wallpapered walls. She slowly follows the sound down a dusty wooden staircase to the pitch-black basement, where a single hanging lightbulb flickers and hums. Suddenly, a shadow moves in the darkness just beyond the reach of the light. She reaches out a trembling hand for the metal pull chain, but the lightbulb suddenly shatters with a loud pop, plunging the room into absolute silence and deep shadows.
