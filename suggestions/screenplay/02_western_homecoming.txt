An aging, weather-beaten gunslinger rides his horse back to an abandoned, dilapidated wooden homestead. Under a warm golden-hour sunset, he kneels beside a weathered wooden grave marker in the high grass. He removes his hat, bows his head, and gently places a single wildflower on the soil. He stands up slowly, turns, and walks toward the deep red horizon. A young boy in dusty overalls watches him from behind a rock, then runs after him, offering a tin canteen.
