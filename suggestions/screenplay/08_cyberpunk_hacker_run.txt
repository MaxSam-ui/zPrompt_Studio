A cybernetic runner sprints down a wet, dark alleyway reflecting towering holographic advertisements. Overhead, a searchlight from a corporate patrol hovercraft sweeps the brick walls. She slides under a low-hanging steam pipe, leaps over a pile of glowing waste, and climbs a fire escape staircase. The patrol hovercraft closes in, but she leaps across a narrow gap between two skyscrapers, landing safely on a metal roof and disappearing into a ventilation shaft.
