Inside a dimly lit apartment, rain streaks down a large window reflecting flickering red and blue neon signs. A weary detective in a rumpled suit stares at a faded photograph of a missing woman under a single hanging lightbulb. He sips amber whiskey from a glass. The rotary phone rings sharply, breaking the silence. He listens as a mysterious voice whispers an address. He grabs his wet trench coat, steps out into the rain-slicked, neon-lit street, and gets into a black sedan. As he pulls away, a pair of headlights clicks on, and a dark car follows him from a distance.
