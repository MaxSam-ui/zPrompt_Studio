Under the golden light of a misty forest sunrise, a mother red fox watches her three playful cubs wrestling on the dew-covered grass outside their dirt den. One curious cub wanders near a fast-flowing, gurgling stream. Suddenly, a shadow passes as a large hawk circles in the bright sky. The mother fox barks sharply, quickly herding her cubs back into the dark safety of the den. She stands guard at the entrance, ears twitching to the wind, eyes scanning the sky until the hawk flies away into the pine trees.
