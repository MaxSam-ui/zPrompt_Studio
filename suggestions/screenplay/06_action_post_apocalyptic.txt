A lone scavenger in rusted, plate-metal armor searches for scrap on a ruined, vine-choked concrete highway bridge under a blinding, midday sun. Suddenly, a heavy metallic clank echoes through the girders. The scavenger instantly draws a custom harpoon pistol, ducking behind the rusted shell of a collapsed yellow school bus. With a deafening engine roar, a massive, heavily armored truck covered in steel spikes and spewing black exhaust smoke bursts through a concrete barricade, throwing up a thick cloud of red dust and debris.
