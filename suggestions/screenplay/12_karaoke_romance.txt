Inside a crowded, neon-lit karaoke bar filled with swirling pink and blue lights, a shy young man is laughingly pushed onto the wooden stage by his friends. He nervously grips the chrome microphone and begins singing an 80s power ballad off-key but with raw emotion. In the front row, a smiling woman steps up, grabs a second mic, and joins him in perfect, warm harmony. They lock eyes, the entire crowd erupts in cheers, and they finish the song laughing and high-fiveing.
