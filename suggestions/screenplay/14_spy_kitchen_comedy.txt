A clumsy secret agent in a sleek tuxedo tries to disarm a ticking bomb placed on a stainless-steel counter in a fancy restaurant kitchen. He accidentally knocks over a stack of copper pots, slips on a wet patch of floor, and sends a giant tray of champagne glasses crashing down with a loud smash. The digital timer on the bomb hits zero, but a miniature flag pops out saying 'Bang!'. From the doorway, his stern female boss watches, shakes her head in disbelief, and then bursts into loud laughter.
