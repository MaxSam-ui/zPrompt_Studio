A squad of heavily armored space marines breaches the airlock of a derelict spaceship, emergency red klaxons flashing. Their helmet-mounted searchlights cut through the floating dust of the pitch-black corridors. Suddenly, metallic alien parasites burst from the overhead ventilation shafts, screeching. The marines open fire with pulse rifles, bright muzzle flashes illuminating the steel walls as hot sparks fly and steam vents. One marine is suddenly snagged by a slimy tentacle and dragged backward into a dark corridor, screaming into his radio.
