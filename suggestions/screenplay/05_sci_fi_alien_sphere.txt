On a barren, dust-swept alien planet, a scientific research team in bulky white spacesuits discovers a perfectly smooth, monolithic black sphere hovering silently above the cracked ground. A scientist slowly reaches out with a gloved hand and touches the cold surface. The sphere suddenly splits open like a mechanical iris, revealing a swirling, glowing purple galaxy inside. A localized gravitational pull drags the scientist toward the opening. The other researchers desperately grab his legs, pulling him back against the heavy gravity until the sphere snaps shut, leaving only silent dust.
