Inside a damp, neon-lit basement net-cafe, a hacker wearing a glowing data-visor types rapidly on a floating holographic keyboard. On her screens, a complex security firewall shatters into digital rain. Suddenly, two armed security drones smash through the glass windows, lasers scanning. She rips a glowing blue data chip from the console, runs out into a crowded, rain-slicked neon street market, and dives into the passenger door of a waiting flying car that blasts off into the sky.
